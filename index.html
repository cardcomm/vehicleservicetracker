About the App

Vehicle Service Tracker helps you keep a clear, private record of your vehicles, service records, mileage, costs, notes, and attachments. Whether you maintain one daily driver or several vehicles, the app gives you a structured place to track what was done, when it was done, who did it, and what it cost.

Create vehicle records, add service history, attach receipts or reference images, and review past maintenance without digging through paper folders or scattered notes. Service records can include dates, mileage, total cost, summaries, detailed notes, service providers, and related attachments.

Vehicle Service Tracker is designed for practical long-term ownership records. Search and filtering tools help you quickly find past repairs, recurring maintenance, high-cost service items, or records for a specific vehicle.

Export and import tools make it easier to back up your data or move records between installs. The app is built around standard, readable backup files so your vehicle history is not trapped in a proprietary format.

Your data stays under your control. Vehicle Service Tracker is built for private, local record keeping on your Mac.
