Getting Started

Welcome to Vehicle Service Tracker! This guide walks you through the basics of creating vehicle records, adding service history, attaching receipts or images, and reviewing your maintenance history over time.

1. Add Your First Vehicle

Start by adding a vehicle record. Enter basic details such as year, make, model, trim, VIN, license plate, current odometer, and any notes you want to keep with the vehicle.

2. Add Service Records

Once a vehicle exists, you can add service records for maintenance, repairs, inspections, parts replacement, or any other work you want to track. Records can include the service date, mileage, total cost, summary, detailed notes, and who performed the work.

3. Attach Receipts, Images, or PDFs

Service records can include attachments such as receipts, invoices, photos, or PDF documents. This helps keep supporting documents connected to the exact service event they belong to.

You can open the attachment viewer from a service record to review saved files later.

4. Search and Review Your History

Use the search and filter tools to find records by vehicle, date range, mileage range, cost range, keyword, or service description. Sorting tools help you review records by date, mileage, cost, or summary.

5. View Reports

Vehicle Service Tracker includes report-style views that summarize vehicle and service information in a clean, printable format. Reports can reflect your current search criteria and sort order.

6. Back Up Your Data

Use the import and export tools to create a backup of your vehicle records, service records, and attachment metadata. Exported data is designed to be readable and restorable.

7. Sample Data

Want to explore the app before entering your own vehicle history?
You can load sample vehicles and service records from the app when sample data options are available.

When you're ready, you can remove sample data — or all data — by visiting the Clearing Your Data section.

Adding and Managing Vehicles

Vehicle records are the foundation of the app. Each service record belongs to a vehicle, so start by creating the vehicles you want to track.

Vehicle details may include:

  • Year, make, model, and trim
  • VIN and license plate
  • Current odometer reading
  • Notes about ownership, condition, or reminders

To update an existing vehicle, select it from the vehicle list and edit the fields in the detail area. Changes are saved through the app’s normal persistence workflow.

Deleting a vehicle may also remove its related service history, depending on the app’s current delete behavior. Review records carefully before removing a vehicle.

Adding and Managing Service Records

Service records track the actual work performed on each vehicle. Use them for routine maintenance, repairs, inspections, parts replacement, diagnostic visits, or any other vehicle-related event.

A service record may include:

  • Service date
  • Mileage at the time of service
  • Total cost
  • Short summary
  • Detailed description or notes
  • Service provider or person who performed the work
  • Attachments such as receipts, invoices, images, or PDFs

Examples of service records include oil changes, tire rotations, brake work, batteries, belts, filters, suspension repairs, inspections, and major repairs.

Keeping mileage and dates accurate makes the history more useful when planning future service or reviewing ownership costs.

Working With Attachments

Attachments let you keep supporting documents connected to the correct service record. This is useful for receipts, invoices, parts labels, photos of damage, warranty documents, or PDF service summaries.

When a service record has attachments, the app can show an attachment indicator so you can quickly see which records include supporting files.

Use the attachment viewer to open saved images or PDF documents from a service record. Attachments are part of your service history and should be included in your backup workflow when exporting data.

Searching, Filtering, and Sorting

Vehicle Service Tracker includes flexible search, filtering, and sorting tools to help you find the records you need.

You can search service records by keyword and narrow results using criteria such as vehicle, service date, mileage, cost, and service text. This is useful for finding a specific repair, reviewing costs over a time period, or locating records related to a part or service provider.

Sorting lets you reorder table results by fields such as date, mileage, cost, or summary. The app keeps sorting behavior consistent between the main service record table and report views where applicable.

These tools are especially useful when you have years of service history and want to review only a subset of records.

Viewing Reports

Reports provide a clean, reviewable summary of your vehicle and service data.

Reports can be used to review service history, summarize ownership costs, prepare information for sale, or print a readable maintenance history for your own records.

Depending on your current criteria, reports may reflect filtered service records, selected vehicles, date ranges, mileage ranges, and sort order.

The report layout is designed to be readable on screen and suitable for printing or saving through standard macOS options.

Exporting and Importing Data

Access import and export options through the app’s import/export tools.

Vehicle Service Tracker lets you back up and transfer your vehicle, service record, and attachment metadata data.

  • Export data to a backup folder
  • Include attachment files when creating a complete backup
  • Import data from a compatible backup folder

Where Exported Files Go

When exporting, the app creates a folder containing the backup JSON file and, when applicable, an attachments folder.

This folder may contain:

  • Your exported JSON backup file
  • Attachment files, if included in the export

Importing Files

When importing, choose the exported backup folder. The app will locate the compatible backup file and restore the included records.

Import may replace existing data depending on the selected import option. Review import choices carefully before continuing.

Clearing Your Data

You can remove sample data — or any data you've entered — when you need to reset the app.

Use the app’s clear database tools to remove:

  • Vehicle records
  • Service records
  • Attachments

⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

App Settings

Vehicle Service Tracker settings may include options for app behavior, appearance, import/export workflows, and sample data tools.

  • Appearance and Theme Options
    Use app style options where available to adjust the look of the interface.
  • Sample Data
